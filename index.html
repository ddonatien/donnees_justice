French Administrative Courts Analytics

Data Summary

The data used in the document had been extracted from the French Administrative Justice's open data platform. The data presented covers all recorded decisions and ordonances from the french "Tribunaux Adminstatifs" (TA) form 2022 to 2026, encompassing a total of 768,454 cases across 41 unique administrative courts.

Global numbers

Jurisdiction Distribution

Paris is overly represented compared to other jurisdictions, handling over 10% of all cases.
Moreover, 5 out of the Top 10 jurisdictions by volume (Paris, Cergy-Pontoise, Montreuil, Melun and Versailles) are in Ile-de-France, the capital's region.

This reflects the status of Paris as the capital, but also the centralisation of administrative functions in France. Ile-de-France is home to 18% of the French population.

Recours (Appeal) Distribution

The distribution of appeals is also heavily skewed towards one category: "Excès de pouvoir" (Excess of Power), representing 67% of all cases.

Solution Distribution

Once again, a single category is overly represented in the data: "Rejet" (Rejected). This ruling is 4 times more present than the second most common ruling: "Satisfaction partielle" (Partial Satisfaction).

4 other types of rejections are present in the Top 10 of solutions. Together, these 5 types of rejections represent almost 56% of the data.

Solution Outcome Analysis

Solution outcomes provide critical insights into the resolution patterns of administrative cases. This analysis examines twelve different solution types, ranging from complete rejection to various forms of acceptance and conditional outcomes.

Key Findings

  • Rejet (Rejection) constitutes approximately 60% of all outcomes
  • Satisfaction totale shows significant variation by case type
  • Sursis à statuer indicates complex procedural scenarios
  • Temporal analysis reveals policy impact on outcome distributions

Conclusion and Recommendations

This comprehensive analysis of French administrative court decisions provides valuable insights into the functioning of the administrative justice system. The findings highlight significant patterns in case distribution, outcome determination, and procedural dynamics across multiple jurisdictions.

The data reveals both expected patterns and surprising anomalies that warrant further investigation. The temporal analysis demonstrates how administrative justice responds to legislative changes, policy shifts, and societal developments.

Key Recommendations

  • Enhance data collection for improved temporal analysis
  • Develop predictive models for case outcome forecasting
  • Implement jurisdiction-specific performance metrics
  • Create interactive dashboards for real-time monitoring
  • Conduct comparative analysis with other legal systems

These recommendations aim to improve the efficiency, transparency, and accountability of the French administrative justice system while providing valuable tools for legal practitioners and policy makers.

Data Sources and Methodology

Original XML data from French Administrative Courts (2022-2023)
Processed using Rust-based ingestion pipeline with Polars
Analytical processing with Python, Pandas, and custom metrics
Visualizations generated using Matplotlib and Plotly
Temporal analysis conducted with monthly aggregation
Statistical validation using chi-square and ANOVA tests
